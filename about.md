Hello! I am **embedded** from [justCatTheFish](https://ctftime.org/team/33893).

I play CTFs, mostly **pwn** and binary exploitation. This is where the writeups
go — the long ones, with the decompiled listings and the parts where I spent
three hours looking for the correct bytes to edit.

Everything here is written in markdown and built with Jekyll. The colors are
generated from the same palette that themes my `nvim`, `tmux` and `zsh` — see
[interdotensional](https://github.com/embe221ed/interdotensional).
