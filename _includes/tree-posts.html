{%- comment -%} `tree posts/` — the homepage index. The guides are real tree output: `├── ` for a sibling that has one after it, `└── ` for the last, and `│ ` / ` ` carried down for each level above. Each row therefore holds its COMPLETE prefix rather than relying on nesting, which is what tree itself prints and what makes a row copy-pasteable on its own. They are aria-hidden: the shape is a picture of the hierarchy, and a screen reader gets that from the nested lists instead. Box Drawing is 100% covered by the shipped Maple Mono — verified against the woff2 — so these are the real characters and not a CSS imitation. Years are directories because that is how _posts/ is actually organised on disk once there is more than one. site.posts is newest-first, so the years come out descending without a sort. Drafts are a second root, not a branch of the first: they live in _drafts/, tree takes more than one path, and `tree posts/ drafts/` is what someone would really type. The command line above adapts, so with no drafts the second root is never claimed. posts/ └── 2022 └── [2022-07-15] writeup madcore 1 directory, 1 file {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign years = site.posts | group_by_exp: "p", "p.date | date: '%Y'" -%} {%- assign ndrafts = site.data.drafts | size -%} {%- assign nposts = site.posts | size -%} {%- assign ndirs = years | size -%}
